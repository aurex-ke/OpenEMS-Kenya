# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Open-Source EMS Pilot Programme Kenya (OpenEMS Kenya) welcomes
participation from people with different backgrounds, experience, and
perspectives. We are committed to making project spaces respectful, inclusive,
and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and collaboration.
- Critique ideas and work constructively; do not attack or demean people.
- Welcome questions, differing viewpoints, and good-faith corrections.
- Use clear, inclusive language and respect people's identities and privacy.
- Keep feedback relevant to the project and its goals.
- Follow project guidance for protecting personal, confidential, and
  security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, and personal attacks are not
acceptable. This includes discriminatory or sexualized language or imagery,
deliberate disruption, unwanted attention, doxxing, and publishing private
information without permission. Retaliation against someone for raising a
concern or participating in an investigation is also not acceptable.

## Where this applies

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
discussions, project events, and other forums when someone is representing
OpenEMS Kenya. It applies equally to participants, maintainers, and project
representatives.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the project
maintainers privately through the communication channel where the project is
hosted. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Include what happened, where and
when it happened, and any relevant links or evidence, if it is safe to do so.

If the concern involves a maintainer, contact another project maintainer or the
hosting platform's reporting channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical. The maintainers will review reports fairly and may ask for
additional information. Please do not use public project issues for urgent
personal safety or security reports.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code.
They will consider the nature and impact of the behavior and may take
proportionate action, including a warning, removal of content, temporary
restriction from project spaces, or permanent exclusion from the project.
Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to
protect participants or the project.

## Acknowledgment

This code of conduct is specific to OpenEMS Kenya and is informed by common
open-source community standards.
