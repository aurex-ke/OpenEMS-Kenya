# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report suspected security vulnerabilities privately using GitHub's
[Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/aurex-ke/OpenEMS-Kenya/security/advisories/new)
feature. This creates a private security advisory visible to the repository
maintainers. Do not report vulnerabilities in public issues, discussions, or
pull requests.

If GitHub's private reporting form is unavailable, contact the repository owner
through [GitHub](https://github.com/aurex-ke) and ask for a private reporting
channel. Do not include vulnerability details in a public message.

When reporting, include the affected file, component, or configuration; the
potential impact; steps to reproduce or a proof of concept where safe; and any
known mitigations. Please do not access, alter, or disrupt systems or data that
you do not own or have explicit permission to test. Avoid including real
credentials, personal data, or sensitive site and operational information.

## What to expect

Maintainers will aim to acknowledge reports within five business days and will
keep the reporter informed as the issue is assessed. We will work with the
reporter on a reasonable disclosure timeline and coordinate public disclosure
after a fix or mitigation is available. Please allow maintainers time to
investigate before disclosing a vulnerability publicly.

## Scope and support

This project is in an early stage and does not currently publish supported
software releases. Reports concerning project-controlled code, scripts,
configuration examples, or documentation are welcome, especially issues that
could affect the security, privacy, or safe operation of an EMS deployment.
Third-party products and services are outside the project's control; please
report vulnerabilities in those products to their respective maintainers as
well.
